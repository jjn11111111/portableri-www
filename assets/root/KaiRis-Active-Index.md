## Epistemic routing

Classify material before synthesis:

- `A — Primary source`: original authored or submitted material
- `B — Process record`: transcript, test, audit, or change record
- `C — Synthesis`: assistant-created connection or protocol
- `D — Placeholder`: named artifact without sufficient source content
- `E — Hypothesis or metaphor`: spiritual, cosmological, or scientific-extension claim not empirically established

A centroid, mean, cluster center, or consensus estimate is a search coordinate. It is not proof of truth. Every result must retain:

- Source links
- Confidence and uncertainty
- Material counterevidence
- Relevant outliers
- Assumptions and weighting choices
- A path back to the original record
