### Cultural training · the shame drawer

Modern public training often treats **myth** as what **record** outgrew: childish, naive, ignoramus — the flights of a “crazy” person once serious people have facts and credentials.

That contempt is **social technology**. It keeps personal and community symbol in a **shame drawer** while **authorized** storytellers (state, brand, platform, documentary-as-truth) use myth at scale without the same insult.

| Taught | Actually |
|--------|----------|
| Myth = immature | Adults still run on myth; it is renamed **mission, identity, news, “the science,”** fandom |
| Sanity = no metaphor | Sanity without record becomes **mood with a logo** |
| Your symbols = crazy | **Unlabeled** myth posing as proof is the failure mode — not symbol itself |

**Lawful myth** is not the opposite of intelligence. **Unlabeled myth insisting it is already proven** is what gets called crazy. **Named myth with a record floor** is what cultures called **wisdom**.

### Heartbeat · local culture · civilization

**Myth is the heartbeat of unique culture** — art, creation, and covenant at the **local** level of community: hearth, studio, Family seat, neighborhood, guild, parish, maker bench.

It is the **basic building block of greater civilization** — not because every local story is globally true, but because **civilization that survives** stacks **distinct local hearts** (myth) under **shared repair** (record): integration, not assimilation.

| Scale | Myth does | Record does |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Local / HOST** | Names what must matter here · art · vow · seat | Export bundle · hash · append-only correction |
| **Network / FNEC** | Gather without hive · cells admit by verify | FAM · no valid map → no sync |
| **Civilization** | Many cultures, not one melted voice | Lawful trade of artifacts, not silent overwrite of origins |

PortAbleRI / **Pari** protects that stack: **sovereign studio** so local myth is not **extracted** into portal memory; **verify and export** so local record can **meet** the wider world without being **erased**.
