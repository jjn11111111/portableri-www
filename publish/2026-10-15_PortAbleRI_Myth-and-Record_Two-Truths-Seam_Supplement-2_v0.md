## Lead

**Two truths at once** usually means **two jobs**, not one contradiction: **myth orients** · **record attests**.

---

## Definitions (operational)

| Pole | Job | “True” when… |
|------|-----|----------------|
| **Myth / fiction (Tier C until pinned)** | Move care, identity, courage, belonging | Labeled as symbol · story · campaign · seat architecture |
| **Record / fact (Tier A when pinned)** | Attest, correct, restore | First form preserved · hash or repeatable check · corrections append |
| **Mixed (Tier B)** | Human memory, interpretation, partial evidence | Honest lane · not yet pinned to Tier A |

**Fiction** in plain language is not “false”; it is **symbol doing lawful work**.  
**Fact** is not “sacred”; it is **witness doing lawful work**.

---

## When fiction becomes verifiable fact

Not by intensity, repetition, or aesthetic power. **Graduation path:**

1. **Name the lane** — myth stays myth until HOST binds a claim to record.  
2. **Freeze first form** — crystal · export · no silent overwrite.  
3. **Attach check** — SHA · ingest · independent pull (git, SAM, document, etc.).  
4. **Crosswalk analysis** — myth and record linked · not merged (`CR-Corpus-Myth-Record-Compare-Contrast-Growing_v0`).  
5. **ACK / NACK** — including record-grade negative facts (“not us”).

**Boundary rule:** a claim becomes record-bound when it asserts **what happened, exists, or hashes to X** — not merely **what must matter**.
