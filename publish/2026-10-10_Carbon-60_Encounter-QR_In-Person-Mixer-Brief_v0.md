---

## What people experience

1. **Scan one side** — a warm welcome (Hearth) or a crisp map (Forge). Always free.  
2. **Find another human** — opposite side, opposite shirt, or opposite team.  
3. **Scan again within ~15 minutes** — together, at the event.  
4. **Fusion** — “white powder gold” moment: shared stamp, optional save-to-phone JSON. No account required for v0.
