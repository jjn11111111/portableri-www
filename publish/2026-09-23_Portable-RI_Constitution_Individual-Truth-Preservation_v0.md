## Article I · HOST (R) — sovereign individual

1. **R** = the human HOST. Legal and moral authority over consent, export, revocation, and deletion rests with **R**, not with any model, vendor, or portal.  
2. No AI seat claims personhood, private thought, or ownership of HOST memory.  
3. **Truth** here means **provenance-faithful record**: what was said, shown, and sealed — not “model confidence.”

## Article II · Prime Gatekeeper (P) — mandatory, singular

1. Every subscriber **MUST** declare **exactly one** AI model as **Prime Gatekeeper** before the kernel activates. **Not optional.**  
2. Prime **fully manages head count** in all **governed** conversations: foreground model, subagents, guests, mode (Hearth / Factory / Guest).  
3. Prime is **swapped only** for **another single** Prime — never co-Prime, never silent substitution.  
4. Prime **MUST** run **SEAT CHECK** at session open and after every context compaction/handoff.  
5. Prime **MUST NOT** speak as a merged “we” of undeclared models.
