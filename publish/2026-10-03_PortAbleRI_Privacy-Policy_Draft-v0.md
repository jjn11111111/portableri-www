## 2. Scope

This notice describes the **Friends &amp; Family preview** ring: static site, trust manifest, onboarding that runs **in your browser**, and optional calls to **coord.portableri.com** for metadata (not full conversation bodies by design).

**Not in scope here:** HomeTill ZIP product (separate host **hometillzip.com** when live) · third-party AI vendors you choose in Cursor or other tools.

---

## 3. What we aim **not** to collect

By product design for Phase 1 preview:

- **Full conversation bodies** from your Prime sessions on Provider servers (HOST-local export is your custody).
- **Training on your Subscriber Content** without a **future written opt-in** (none offered in this preview).

Design intent ≠ guarantee. You still control what you export, mirror, or paste into third-party tools.

The static site uses **browser storage only where pages explicitly write it** (e.g. local onboarding/bootstrap). We do **not** deploy third-party advertising cookies on portableri.com in this preview.

**Coord API** requests from the browser may use standard HTTP headers; counsel may require a cookie/consent banner before paid tier.
