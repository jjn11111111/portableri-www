## Tessellation on fabric & bags

1. **Honeycomb field** — repeating **hex** print (tone-on-tone) across shirt or tote.  
2. **Pent portal** — **one pentagon** (contrasting color) chest or back · **QR inside the pent** — “scan the portal.”  
3. **Double-sided** — hex side = lore · pent side = encounter pair (Hearth/Forge voices still work).  
4. **Inside lining** — hidden hex tessellation · pent QR on inner tag for “you had to open it together.”  
5. **Misregistration is OK** — slight print drift reads handmade; QR stays inside quiet zone on the pent only.

## Scan types (URL layer)

Extend encounter URLs with optional **`tile=`**:

| `tile` | Role | Voice default |
|--------|------|----------------|
| `hex` | Common stamp · mixer loop | Hearth-friendly copy |
| `pent` | Fusion · steward · 1/1 serial | Forge-friendly copy |

Legacy **`face=hearth|forge`** remains (dual door). **`tile=hex|pent`** is the **world’s-game** skin for the same state machine.

Example:

```
…/encounter/demo.html?serial=C60-…&tile=hex
…/encounter/demo.html?serial=C60-…&tile=pent&pair=…
```
