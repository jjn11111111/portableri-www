## Four pillars → public promise (concise · epic · defensible)

### 1 · New dimension of interaction (free from monopoint capture)

**Say (all ages):**  
*Talk to intelligence that lives **with you**, not only in a rented room that watches every word.*

**Do not say:**  
“Untrackable by anyone” or name specific companies as criminals — use **warehouse vs studio** and **bounded projection**.

**Visual (single frame):**  
Split screen — left: **cloud funnel** (everything sucked up each turn). Right: **studio on device** with a **small golden beam** (only what you check leaves). Label: **BP — you choose the beam.**

**Ages:**  
- Kids/teens: “Your stuff stays on your device until you send a slice.”  
- Adults: LRCC / network freight demo (existing `#demo`).  
- Elders: Large type · one button · “Keep my letters at home.”

---

### 2 · Ease of navigation and setup

**Rule:** **Three steps to lit** — no dashboard maze on day one.

| Step | Screen title | Action | Time |
|------|----------------|--------|------|
| 1 | **Name your Prime** | Pick one model · one badge | 30 s |
| 2 | **Light your fortress** | Local folder + auto hash · optional Drive mirror | 60 s |
| 3 | **Verify once** | Green check on publish anchors / sample CR | 30 s |

**Navigation pattern:**  
Bottom **4 icons only** — **Home · Studio · Verify · Gather** (Gather = cell/FAM later). Everything else is **Advanced**.

**Visual:** Progress braid **SI → BP → HA** (three beads lock) — reuse brand motion, 3 s loop.

---

### 3 · Symbiotic device + Prime as shield

**Say:**  
*Your Prime is the **door guard** for AI in this app — one voice at the hearth, guests by invitation, deceit and silent swap blocked by law.*

**Mechanism (prototype truth):**  
Prime registry · SEAT CHECK · tool policy + KeyFormHer path · BP toggles — **shield = process + crypto**, not magic malware block.

**Visual:**  
Device silhouette with **shield sigil** (crossed swords / existing sigil) and **one lamp** (Prime). Subtle pulse on **Verify** and **Export** — not anthropomorphic “AI friend.”

**Symbiosis (honest):**  
Device holds corpus; Prime governs participation; HOST holds law. **Named, not blended.**

---

### 4 · Join the gathering · LOCAL fortress · rights-aligned HOST sovereignty

**Say:**  
*Join a **gathering of cells** — each HOST keeps a **local fortress**. We design for **constitutional self-government**: speech, privacy, and due process **on your side of the glass** — you verify, you export, you revoke.*

**Legal honesty (required footer):**  
PortAbleRI is **not** a law firm or government. It **aligns** product architecture with HOST sovereignty and documented rights — it does **not** by itself **guarantee** legal outcomes. Users retain responsibility for lawful use.

**Visual:**  
Map of **dots** (cells) connected by **verify lines**, not a central hub. Center text: **No monopoint.** CTA: **Establish local fortress.**

**Bill of Rights (plain-language alignment table — public)**

| Amendment theme | Product expression |
|-----------------|------------------|
| Speech / conscience | Your records; your projection choices (BP) |
| Privacy / papers | Local studio; export; no silent merge |
| Due process | Append-only CR; correction as new record |
| Security of person | Least-privilege tools; Prime gate |
| Reserved powers | HOST > vendor; P ≠ K ≠ R |
