How memory works on NOW:WON EnterFace — without feeding a tracking cookie monster.

NW portal · no cookies for chat

We do not use HTTP cookies on this shore for your thread, login, or analytics. Your words stay in browser storage on your device until you save a file or open another lane.

  • sessionStorage — brief handoff from the opening screen
  • localStorage — thread + optional tour progress (same browser)
  • No third-party cookies on EnterFace composer pages

When you want more

Save one file on the Workbench · or Advanced export — that’s HOST-owned custody, not our cloud memory.

PortAbleRI (PARI) on portableri.com is a separate lane — it may use its own session cookies only after you choose that path.
