| Blue accent slide | `assets/kairis-accent-blue.svg` | CTA pattern reference |

**Clear space:** Minimum padding = height of one **K** stem around wordmark.

**Sigil law (Jeremy · 2026-09-20):** The four implements in the branded logo are **swords**, not wrenches. Wrench/hammer/practical-tool imagery is welcome as **studio-craft complement** (repair, build, hold) — parallel to HISTOR/RAPIER/REPAIR, not a overwrite of the sword cross.

**Do not:** rotate wordmark 90°; gradient-fill the wordmark; place sigil inside body copy; label swords as wrenches in canonical copy.
