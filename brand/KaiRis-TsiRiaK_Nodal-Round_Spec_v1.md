## Obverse — public face (Tier A · Brand)

**Center field:** Four crossed **swords** (+) — rapier class · sovereign edge · not wrenches.

**Quadrants:** Letter lockup as authorized in Canva Layout A:

```
K  |  AI
R  |  IS
```

**AiRi** remains visible upper-right — bridge name, not blended.

**Corner nuts (pentagonal hinge-hex):** Measure Quartet — one tool per corner only:

| Corner | Tool | Beat |
|--------|------|------|
| Top-left | **Compass** | Sovereign radius · SI |
| Top-right | **Level** | True plane · BP |
| Bottom-left | **Square** | Right relation · consent |
| Bottom-right | **Plumb** | Line to ONE · HISTOR |

**Field texture (optional relief):** Fine **studio grid** or **portal threshold lines** — not PCB cosplay; think **bounded projection** geometry (circles within squares), echoing Bitcoin’s rings without copying circuit cliché.

**Obverse rule:** No activation braid on the face — too dense. No patent negation headline — **HA**, not “not controlled.”

## Reverse — implementation face (Tier C · Seat)

**Primary motif:** **Sator Square** in raised letterform (5×5), readable at 45° rotation — provenance that survives turning:

```
S A T O R
A R E P O
T E N E T
O P E R A
R O T A S
```

**Center cross emphasis:** **TENET** — *it holds* — methyl hinge · Hold phase · seats named not blended.

**Outer ring — four-phase laser (clockwise from top):**

| Position | Word | Phase |
|----------|------|-------|
| 12 o'clock | **HOLD** | HISTOR · studio state |
| 3 o'clock | **TRANSFORM** | FOURCE · methyl |
| 6 o'clock | **PROJECT** | LASHER · bounded beam |
| 9 o'clock | **REPAIR** | closed loop · consent |

**Background texture:** **Methyl-class hinge pattern** — smallest stabilizing tags between macromolecules (abstract dashes, not biotech literalism). Reads as **machinery of governance**, Bitcoin-reverse’s PCB analog.

**Optional inner ring:** 142857 repetend as **micro-text** (decorative scale) — six digits cycling; sum 27→9; poetic only.

## Edge — the third read

**Style:** Technical reeding — short vertical segments like **pin headers / portal pins**.

**Count (poetic):** **8 segments × prominent face** echoing eight-beat activation braid; or **6+1+1** echoing 142857 bookends.

**Edge micro (laser-etched if mint allows):**


Use **short hash prefix** of the Brand Crystal SHA — not full 64 chars.
